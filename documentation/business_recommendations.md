# Business Recommendations – E-Commerce Returns Analytics

## 1. Improve Clothing Product Quality and Sizing Information
**Finding:** Clothing has the highest return rate at approximately 37.3%.

**Recommendation:**
- Provide detailed product size charts.
- Add accurate product descriptions and images.
- Review customer feedback for frequently returned products.
- Improve quality checks before dispatch.

**Expected business benefit:** Reduce avoidable clothing returns and improve customer satisfaction.

## 2. Strengthen Product Quality Control
**Finding:** Defective products account for 382 recorded returns.

**Recommendation:**
- Introduce stricter product inspections.
- Monitor suppliers with recurring quality issues.
- Track defective return rates by product and category.

**Expected business benefit:** Reduce defective-product returns and associated costs.

## 3. Improve Product Listings and Order Accuracy
**Finding:** Wrong Item accounts for 348 recorded returns.

**Recommendation:**
- Improve product descriptions and specifications.
- Verify product identification during packaging.
- Introduce barcode-based order verification.

**Expected business benefit:** Reduce incorrect deliveries and unnecessary reverse logistics.

## 4. Analyze Return-Related Financial Costs
**Finding:** Returned orders create additional recorded return-related costs.

**Recommendation:**
- Monitor return costs by product category.
- Identify categories with high return costs.
- Review return policies and reverse logistics processes.

**Expected business benefit:** Improve cost control and operational efficiency.

## 5. Monitor Environmental Impact
**Finding:** The dataset records CO2 emissions, packaging waste, CO2 saved, and waste avoided.

**Recommendation:**
- Monitor emissions and packaging waste by category.
- Explore more efficient packaging options.
- Track sustainability indicators over time.

**Expected business benefit:** Support better environmental monitoring and more sustainable operations.

## Conclusion
These recommendations use the project's SQL analysis and Tableau dashboard findings to identify opportunities for improving product quality, reducing returns, controlling costs, and monitoring environmental impact.
