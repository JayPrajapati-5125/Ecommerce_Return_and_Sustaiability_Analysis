# Business Insights – E-Commerce Returns Analytics

## 1. Sales Performace
- from january 2022-august 2025, get 4984 orders with average of ₹2,367 order value.
- Clothing has highest order volumn and revenue.
- Electronics is second most selling category.
- Home Appliances have a lowest order volumn and revenue.

**Insight:** Clothing and Electronic are most selling category in products.

## 2. Product Category Return Performance

- Clothing has the highest return rate at approximately 37.3%.
- Home Appliances have a return rate of approximately 25.6%.
- Electronics have a return rate of approximately 25.2%.
- Toys have a return rate of approximately 24%.
- Books have a return rate of approximately 22.5%.

**Insight:** Clothing has a notably higher return rate than the other analyzed categories and deserves further investigation.

## 3. Return Reasons

The major recorded return reasons are:
- Defective: 382 orders
- Changed Mind: 379 orders
- Wrong Item: 348 orders
- Size Issue: 341 orders

**Insight:** Defective products and changed customer preferences are the two most frequent recorded return reasons.

## 4. Sales and Order Trends

The monthly sales trend dashboard helps identify changes in order value and sales performance over time.

**Insight:** Monthly trends can help management identify periods of stronger or weaker sales activity.

## 5. Financial Impact

The project examines order value and return-related costs to understand the financial impact of returned orders.

**Important:** Profit_Loss in this dataset is defined as Order_Value minus Return_Cost. It should not be interpreted as complete accounting profit.

## 6. Sustainability Analysis

The dashboard compares recorded CO2 emissions and packaging waste across product categories.

**Insight:** These metrics help compare the recorded environmental impact across categories. They should be interpreted according to the dataset's definitions.

## Analysis Scope
- Analysis period: January 2022 – August 2025
- Orders analyzed: 4,984
- September 2025 was excluded because it contains only three days of data.
